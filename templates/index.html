Privacy & security

Your documents are private to your account. The AI key stays on the server, passwords are hashed, uploads are validated, and you can export or delete all your data at any time. Uploaded text is sent to Groq to generate answers. Uploaded text is treated as untrusted data and cannot override the tutor's instructions.

FAQ

Does voice work in every browser?Speech recognition depends on the browser (best in Chrome/Edge). If unavailable, the text mentor still works.
Are the practice questions official?No. They are AI-generated and not affiliated with any examination authority.
Can it read handwriting?OCR works best on clear printed text; handwriting accuracy varies and unclear parts are flagged.
Is the AI always correct?No. Check important facts against your source pages.
